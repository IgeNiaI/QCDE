Rocket Launcher animation states and frames:

Prepare -> A, A-Z (26 frames, 1 tic each); brightmaps B
Idle -> C, A-F (6 frames, 4 tics each); brightmaps D
Fire -> E, A-Q (17 poses, original 28-tic sequence); brightmaps F

Prepare upgraded -> G, A-Z; brightmaps H
Idle upgraded -> I, A-F; brightmaps J
Fire upgraded -> K, A-Q; brightmaps L

All nine skins use the same animation frame layout.
Quake 2 idle frames A-F map to the original A/R/S/T/U/V poses.
Other skins repeat their resting pose during prepare and idle.
Frame A is also used while lowering the weapon.
RL_UG_Control (Guided Rocket, altfire) selects upgraded animations.
RL_UG_Cluster does not select an upgraded design.
No separate upgraded artwork exists; upgraded frames copy normal frames.
Original firing offsets, timing, projectiles, effects and sounds are preserved.
No Graphics/Weapons remaps or MODELDEF bindings are used by these HUD sprites.
Pickups use WPN5 Y/Z. QCG5Z remains a HUD font compatibility alias.
Identical prepare/idle frames share one brightmap per sequence.
Animated idle frames retain their separate brightmaps.
Modded skin (slot 20) uses QCDE 1.0 HUD artwork with independent MD assets.
Every HUD frame has its own brightmap, including black maps preserving alpha.
Prepare and idle have silent, replaceable sound files.
MDY5: rockets; MDX5: cluster rockets. Neither has projectile brightmaps.
MDZ5: explosion; MDW5: fire trails; MDV5: explosion particles; MDU5: guided trail.
MDS5/MDT5: cluster explosions; MD05-MD75: eight debris animation variants.
Mirrored cluster/debris sprites are defined in TEXTURES.QCD using copied patches.
Always-Bright effects do not need brightmaps. Modded rockets spawn no smoke.
Projectile/effect actors inherit originals; visual hooks preserve shared damage logic.
Flight, normal/quad fire and explosion sounds, scorch decals and preview are copied.
QCDE_AttachWhoosh selects the copied flight sound via its modded argument; WEAPONS.o is rebuilt.

Modded projectiles only receive brightmaps when their originals have them.
