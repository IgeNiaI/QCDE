"QC: Doom Edition" Ivory Duke & Barbarossa Voxels Addon

This is a 100% optional addon. You can load it in your side for
single player or multiplayer, whether the server has it loaded
or not.

All voxel models created by Ivory Duke and Barbarossa, except
where noted otherwise in the README.txt file


====================================================================

"QCDE_UT_Weapons_v3.0_Voxels" Ivory Duke & Pixo

This addon is based on the Voxels addon above, but replaces
original QCDE weapons voxel models with weapons from
Unreal Tournament series

Weapon voxel models created by Pixo, the rest by Ivory Duke and Barbarossa
