QC: Doom Edition "George Exley Announcer"

As the title says, this is an announcer pack, voiced and recorded 
by George Exley.


Additional Credits:
- DBThanatos: Packing, releasing and stuff
- Whoo: ANCRINFO & SNDINFO


====================================================================

* How to use it? *

1. Put the .pk3 file in your Zandronum/Announcer folder
2. In Zandronum go to All Options -> Sound Options -> Announcer
3. Select "George Exley Announcer"
4. Increase "Announcer Volume" (if it was low/off)
5. Profit!
