Super Nailgun animation states and frames:

Prepare			-> A, A-Z (26 frames, 1 tic each)
	brightmaps	-> B
Idle			-> C, A-L (12 frames, 3 tics each)
	brightmaps	-> D
Fire start		-> E, A (resting pose)
	brightmaps	-> F
Fire loop		-> E, B-Q (16 poses)
	brightmaps	-> F
Fire end		-> G, A-R (18 frames, 2 tics each)
	brightmaps	-> H

Upgrade animation sprites (active while SNG_UG_Seeker is owned):
Prepare			-> I, A-Z
	brightmaps	-> J
Idle			-> K, A-L
	brightmaps	-> L
Fire start		-> M, A
	brightmaps	-> N
Fire loop		-> M, B-Q
	brightmaps	-> N
Fire end		-> O, A-R
	brightmaps	-> P

Every skin uses the same animation slots and timing, including fire end.
Frame A is also used when lowering the weapon.
Static idle skins repeat the resting pose; Quake 3 uses its rotating idle.
Quake 3 prepare uses idle poses DDEEEFFFGGGHHHIIIJJJKKKLLL for both
normal and upgraded frames, retaining the 26-tic duration and ending on L
before idle restarts at A. Repeated poses share their prepare brightmap.
Fire end lasts 36 tics and allows A_WeaponReady throughout.
Fire end starts with the former B pose. Former B-R occupy A-Q, and R
repeats the final pose to preserve the 18 slots and 36-tic duration.
Default, Quake 1, QCDE 1.0, ET QW, and Quake 4 retain the progressive
slowdown, with an 8-tic final idle hold. Quake 2 retains its faster initial
rotation and finishes with a 6-tic idle hold.
Three-pose cycles never skip poses, avoiding apparent backward rotation.
All six spin-down skins finish exactly at idle.
Quake 3 continues its idle rotation; Half-Life and Serious Sam remain static.
The same mappings apply to normal and upgraded sprites and brightmaps.
Normal, DM, and homing firing retain their original attack timing.
Skins without dedicated upgraded artwork copy normal frames and brightmaps.
SNG_UG_Lava controls lava projectile behavior independently.
Default skin Graphics assets and TEXTURES.QCD remaps cover every phase.

Modded skin (slot 20):
Based on QCDE 1.0; HUD prefix MD, with a dedicated brightmap per frame.
Normal and upgraded phases have independent files under SuperNailgun/Modded.
NAMD: directional normal, lava, homing, and lava-homing nail sprites A-D.
MDUD: lava flame effect; MDVD: lava flame/trail.
MDWD, MDXD, MDYD, MDZD: four impact variants, each A-T.
Effect brightmaps use MB prefixes only for frames rendered without Bright.
Missing brightmaps are black copies preserving the original alpha.
Always-Bright projectile, impact, and flame frames have no brightmaps.
MDUD T-X retain brightmaps for their non-Bright fade-in; MDVD A retains its
brightmap for the flame burner, even though the lava trail also uses it Bright.
All HUD frames retain their dedicated brightmaps.
Sounds/Weapons/SuperNailgun/Modded includes dedicated prepare and idle silence,
firing, fire end, homing, quad firing/homing, and seven impact sounds.
MDSuperNailgunChip decals use Graphics/Decals/SuperNailgun/MDDchip1-5.png.
All projectile/effect actors use separate Modded assets.
The Modded skin has no muzzle smoke or homing projectile smoke.
The preview is Graphics/Menu/SkinPreviews/SuperNailgun/SKINGDMD.png.
Eleena's illusion also uses the Modded projectile, impact, and firing sounds.
