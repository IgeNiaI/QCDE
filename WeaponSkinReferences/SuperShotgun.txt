Super Shotgun

Assets

Weapon and effect sprites: Sprites/Weapons/SuperShotgun/
Each skin has its own folder. Modded assets are in Modded/.
Animation folders are named by set and use; effect folders are named by effect.
Brightmaps are in the animation/effect subfolders.
Sounds: Sounds/Weapons/SuperShotgun/
Each skin has its own folder; Quad/ holds quad-damage sounds.
Larger source graphics: Graphics/Weapons/SuperShotgun/Default/
Modded models and model textures: MD3/PROJECTILES/SuperShotgun/
Menu previews: Graphics/Menu/SkinPreviews/SuperShotgun/
